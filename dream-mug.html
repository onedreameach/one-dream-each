FAQ

BEFORE YOU ORDER.

Does the mug really change color?

Yes. The base product uses a thermochromic exterior: black at room temperature, progressively white when heated. The printed artwork is revealed as the surface changes.

Is the website preview identical to every finished mug?

The preview uses the real base product references and the actual print artwork, but small differences in print placement, heat pattern, screen color and manufacturing are normal. It is a product preview, not a promise of pixel-perfect placement.

How should I wash it?

Hand wash only. The supplier does not recommend dishwasher use for the magic mug.

How long does delivery take?

Production is typically estimated at 3–5 working days. Carrier transit then depends on the destination and shipping service. The checkout price uses the economy shipping table for supported countries.

Who makes and ships it?

The mug is produced on demand by a specialist print-on-demand partner and shipped directly after production. One Dream Each supplies the artwork and customer-facing product experience.
