Refund Policy

This Policy explains when a OneDreamEach payment may be reviewed, completed or refunded, and how refund requests are handled.

LAST UPDATED · 13 AUGUST 2026
Need help with a payment?

Contact: onedreameach@gmail.com

Please include the relevant Dream number or payment date where possible. Do not send complete payment-card details by email.

1. The service

OneDreamEach provides an online service that allows a user to submit a Dream for publication on the OneDreamEach public archive.

The current price is €1 per Dream unless another price is clearly displayed before checkout.

Following successful payment, the service is designed to begin processing the Dream immediately and, when successful, assign a unique Dream number, create a public Dream Page and make a Story Card available.

2. Immediate performance and withdrawal rights

Before completing payment, users are asked to expressly request that performance of the service begins during the applicable withdrawal period.

Users are also asked to acknowledge that, once the service has been fully performed, they may lose their statutory right of withdrawal to the extent provided by applicable consumer law.

For OneDreamEach, full performance will normally occur when the purchased Dream has been successfully created and published with its unique Dream number and public Dream Page.

Nothing in this Refund Policy removes or limits any consumer right that cannot legally be excluded or limited.

3. Successfully completed Dreams

Where payment has been successfully completed, the user requested immediate performance and the Dream has been successfully created and published, the service will generally be treated as completed.

In those circumstances, a later change of mind about the purchase or the wording of the Dream does not automatically create a right to a refund.

This is always subject to any mandatory rights that apply under consumer law.

4. Payment taken but Dream not created

If a payment is successfully charged but a technical failure prevents the Dream from being created, please contact OneDreamEach.

After reviewing the transaction, OneDreamEach may, depending on the circumstances:

  • complete the creation or publication of the Dream;
  • correct the relevant technical error; or
  • issue a refund where appropriate.

5. Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you believe the same intended purchase was accidentally charged more than once, or a charge resulted from a technical error, contact OneDreamEach so the transaction can be reviewed.

Confirmed accidental duplicate or erroneous charges may be refunded.

6. Failed, pending or reversed payments

A Dream is not considered successfully purchased until the payment has been confirmed.

A payment that is declined, cancelled, reversed or remains incomplete may not result in a Dream being created.

Where a payment provider or bank displays a temporary authorisation that is later released, this may not constitute a completed charge or a refund issued by OneDreamEach.

7. Dreams removed for violating the Terms

A Dream may be rejected, restricted or removed if it violates the Terms & Conditions, applicable law, third-party rights or legitimate safety and security requirements.

Removal resulting from a violation of the Terms does not automatically entitle the user to a refund.

This does not affect any mandatory consumer rights that apply in the circumstances.

8. Removal requested by the user

A request to remove a Dream after it has been successfully published is separate from a refund request.

Removing a published Dream at the user's request does not automatically reverse the completed service or create a right to reimbursement.

Privacy or legal removal requests will be assessed independently under the applicable Privacy Policy, Terms and law.

9. How to request a payment review

To ask OneDreamEach to review a payment or a possible refund, email:

onedreameach@gmail.com

Where available, include:

  • your Dream number;
  • the approximate payment date;
  • the nickname used for the Dream;
  • a short description of the problem.

For security, never send a full payment-card number, CVV or complete card credentials by email.

10. Approved refunds

If a refund is approved, it will normally be initiated through the original payment method where technically available.

Once a refund has been initiated, the time required for the funds to appear can depend on Stripe, the user's bank, card issuer and payment network.

OneDreamEach cannot control processing times imposed by independent payment providers or financial institutions.

11. Chargebacks and payment disputes

If you believe a transaction is incorrect, contacting OneDreamEach first may allow the issue to be investigated more quickly.

This does not prevent you from exercising any lawful rights available through your payment provider, card issuer or applicable consumer protection rules.

12. Mandatory consumer rights

Nothing in this Policy is intended to exclude, waive or restrict rights that apply under mandatory Italian, European Union or other applicable consumer law.

If a mandatory legal rule gives you a stronger right than this Policy, that mandatory rule will prevail.

13. Changes to this Policy

This Refund Policy may be updated where reasonably necessary to reflect changes to the service, payment process or applicable legal requirements.

The latest update date will be displayed at the top of this page.

Updates do not remove mandatory rights that already apply to completed transactions.
