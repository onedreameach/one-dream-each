Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information OneDreamEach handles, why it is used, which parts of a Dream become public and what privacy rights may apply to you.

LAST UPDATED · 13 AUGUST 2026
Privacy contact

OneDreamEach is operated from Italy. The person operating the project determines the purposes and means of the personal-data processing described in this Policy.

Privacy enquiries and data-rights requests: onedreameach@gmail.com

1. About this Policy

This Policy describes how personal data is collected, used, published, stored and otherwise processed when you visit or use OneDreamEach.

Personal data is handled in accordance with applicable data-protection law, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies.

2. Information you provide

When you submit a Dream, you may provide:

  • a nickname;
  • your Dream or message;
  • your country;
  • an Instagram username, if you choose to provide one;
  • a TikTok username, if you choose to provide one.

Information marked as optional is not required to publish a Dream unless the website indicates otherwise at the time of submission.

3. Dreams are public

OneDreamEach is designed as a public archive. After successful payment and creation of the Dream, information submitted for publication may appear on the Dream Wall, the individual Dream Page, a shareable Story Card and other public areas of the service.

Public information may include your Dream, nickname, country, Dream number and any social usernames that you voluntarily provided.

Please do not submit confidential information, special-category personal data, financial details, passwords, private contact information or anything else that you do not want to become publicly accessible.

Public content may be copied, shared, indexed by search engines or cached by independent third parties. As a result, information may sometimes remain visible outside OneDreamEach even after it has been removed from the OneDreamEach website.

4. Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. OneDreamEach does not directly store your full payment-card details.

Stripe may process payment, transaction, anti-fraud and related information in accordance with its own legal obligations and privacy documentation.

5. Technical and usage information

When you use the website, technical information may be processed automatically in order to deliver, secure, troubleshoot and maintain the service.

Depending on the relevant infrastructure, this can include request data, timestamps, browser or device information, IP-address-related technical data, security logs, referrer information and page or route information.

6. Vercel Web Analytics

OneDreamEach currently uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic and usage.

According to Vercel's current documentation, Vercel Web Analytics is designed without cookies for visitor identification and provides aggregated analytics rather than cross-site tracking of individual visitors.

Analytics data may include information such as page or route viewed, referrer, approximate geographic information, device type, operating system, browser and event timestamp.

OneDreamEach does not currently use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or TikTok Pixel for behavioural advertising tracking.

7. Why information is processed

Information may be processed in order to:

  • receive and process your Dream submission;
  • process and verify payment;
  • create a unique Dream number;
  • publish and display your Dream;
  • generate a public Dream Page and Story Card;
  • operate, measure, secure and improve the website;
  • prevent abuse, fraud and technical misuse;
  • respond to support, removal or privacy requests;
  • establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Legal bases

Depending on the processing activity and the law that applies, processing may be based on:

  • performance of the service or steps requested by you before entering into the transaction;
  • compliance with legal obligations;
  • legitimate interests, such as service security, fraud prevention, troubleshooting and protection of the integrity of the platform;
  • consent, where consent is the appropriate legal basis and is requested from you.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent where applicable without affecting processing that was lawful before the withdrawal.

9. Service providers

OneDreamEach uses third-party technology providers to operate the service. These currently include:

  • Stripe — payment processing;
  • Supabase — database and backend infrastructure;
  • Vercel — website hosting, serverless infrastructure and Web Analytics.

These providers may process personal or technical information where necessary to provide their respective services.

10. Social-media links

You may optionally provide Instagram or TikTok usernames. Where provided, OneDreamEach may make those usernames clickable from your public Dream Page.

Instagram, Meta, TikTok and ByteDance operate independently from OneDreamEach. If you follow an external link, the privacy practices of the relevant third party apply.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

OneDreamEach does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioural advertising pixels.

Essential storage or technical processing may nevertheless be used where necessary for security, payment processing, hosting, delivery of the service or functionality requested by the user.

If non-essential analytics or advertising technologies that require consent are introduced, this Policy and the site's consent mechanisms should be updated before or when those technologies are activated.

12. Data retention

Public Dreams are intended to remain part of the OneDreamEach archive for an extended period, subject to the Terms, moderation, valid removal requests, legal requirements and the continued operation of the service.

Other information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, including security, transaction records, accounting, fraud prevention, support, dispute resolution and legal-compliance requirements.

Different categories of information may therefore have different retention periods.

13. International data transfers

Some service providers may process information in countries outside the European Economic Area.

Where data-protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, the relevant transfer should be handled using an applicable legal mechanism or safeguard provided under the GDPR framework.

14. Your data-protection rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions provided by applicable law, you may have rights including:

  • access to personal data concerning you;
  • correction of inaccurate personal data;
  • deletion of personal data;
  • restriction of processing;
  • data portability where applicable;
  • objection to certain processing;
  • withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.

Requests can be sent to onedreameach@gmail.com .

Reasonable information may be requested to verify that the person making the request is entitled to exercise the relevant right.

15. Removal of a public Dream

If a Dream contains personal data concerning you, or you believe that a Dream violates your privacy or other rights, you may request review by contacting the privacy email above.

Please include the relevant Dream number or URL where possible so that the content can be identified efficiently.

Removal from OneDreamEach cannot guarantee removal of independent copies, screenshots, search-engine caches or content previously shared on third-party platforms.

16. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information handled through OneDreamEach.

No online service, transmission method or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

17. Children and minors

Users should not submit another child's or minor's personal information unless they have the legal authority and any consent or other lawful basis required to do so.

If you believe personal information relating to a minor has been published improperly, please contact OneDreamEach for review.

18. Automated decision-making

OneDreamEach does not currently use personal data to make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects on users.

19. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the service, technology, providers, processing activities or applicable legal requirements.

The latest update date will be shown at the top of this page.

20. Complaints

If you believe that personal data concerning you has been processed unlawfully, you may have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection supervisory authority.

In Italy, the supervisory authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Privacy questions, access requests, deletion requests and other data-protection enquiries can be sent to:
