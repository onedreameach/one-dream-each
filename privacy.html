Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026
One Dream Each is operated by a private individual based in Italy.
Privacy contact: onedreameach@gmail.com

1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used and stored when you visit or use One Dream Each.

One Dream Each respects the privacy of its users and processes personal data in accordance with applicable data protection law, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

2. Data you provide

When you submit a Dream, you may provide:

  • a nickname;
  • your Dream or message;
  • your country;
  • an Instagram username, if provided;
  • a TikTok username, if provided.

Your nickname, Dream, country and any social usernames you choose to provide are intended to be published publicly as part of the One Dream Each project.

You should therefore not include private, confidential or sensitive information in your Dream that you do not want to make public.

3. Public nature of Dreams

One Dream Each is a public platform.

After successful payment, your submitted content may appear on the Dream Wall, your individual Dream page and other public areas of the website.

Public content may be viewed, shared or indexed by search engines and may potentially remain available in search-engine caches or third-party services even after it has been removed from One Dream Each.

4. Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe.

One Dream Each does not directly receive or store your complete payment card number.

Stripe may process payment and transaction information according to its own privacy documentation and applicable legal obligations.

5. Technical data

When you access the website, certain technical information may be processed automatically as necessary to operate, secure and deliver the service.

This may include information such as IP address, request information, browser or device information, timestamps and technical logs.

Some of this information may be processed by infrastructure and hosting providers used by One Dream Each.

6. How we use personal data

Personal data may be processed in order to:

  • process your request to publish a Dream;
  • process and verify payment;
  • publish and display your Dream;
  • provide individual Dream pages;
  • operate and secure the website;
  • prevent abuse, fraud and technical misuse;
  • respond to support or privacy requests;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Legal bases

Depending on the specific processing activity, personal data may be processed because it is necessary to perform the service requested by you, to comply with a legal obligation, or for legitimate interests such as maintaining the security and integrity of the service.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent where applicable, without affecting processing that was lawful before the withdrawal.

8. Service providers

One Dream Each relies on third-party technology providers to operate the service.

These currently include services used for:

  • payment processing — Stripe;
  • database and backend infrastructure — Supabase;
  • website hosting and serverless infrastructure — Vercel.

These providers may process limited personal or technical information as necessary to provide their respective services.

9. Social media links

Users may optionally provide Instagram or TikTok usernames.

One Dream Each may display links that take visitors to those external platforms.

Once you leave One Dream Each and visit a third-party website or social network, the processing of your data is governed by that third party's own privacy practices.

10. Analytics and advertising tracking

At the date of this Privacy Policy, One Dream Each does not intentionally use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or TikTok Pixel for behavioural analytics or advertising tracking.

If analytics or advertising technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy and, where legally required, the site's consent mechanisms will be updated accordingly.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

One Dream Each does not intentionally place advertising or behavioural tracking cookies directly on the website at the date of this Privacy Policy.

Essential technical functionality or third-party infrastructure may nevertheless involve technical storage or processing necessary for security, payment processing or delivery of the service.

12. Data retention

Published Dreams are intended to remain part of the One Dream Each project for an extended period, subject to moderation, legal obligations and valid removal requests.

Other personal or technical information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including security, payment, accounting, dispute resolution and legal compliance requirements.

13. International data transfers

Some technology providers used by One Dream Each may process data in countries outside the European Economic Area.

Where required by applicable data protection law, such transfers should be subject to appropriate safeguards provided by the relevant service provider.

14. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request:

  • access to your personal data;
  • correction of inaccurate data;
  • deletion of personal data;
  • restriction of processing;
  • data portability where applicable;
  • objection to certain processing;
  • withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.

Requests can be sent to: onedreameach@gmail.com .

We may need to request reasonable information to verify that the person requesting access, modification or deletion is entitled to make that request.

15. Removal of a public Dream

If you believe that a Dream contains your personal data or infringes your privacy or other rights, you may contact us to request review or removal.

Requests should identify the relevant Dream number or URL wherever possible.

16. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information handled through One Dream Each.

However, no online service or method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

17. Children's privacy

Users should not submit personal information relating to children or minors unless they have the legal authority and any consent required to do so.

18. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the service, technology, service providers or applicable legal requirements.

The latest update date will be displayed at the top of this page.

19. Complaints

If you believe your personal data has been processed unlawfully, you may have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority.

In Italy, the competent supervisory authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
